CHALLENGER DEEP for the Shanling Q2 (Q2 Pod)
============================================

Sail one of nine ships on one of five seas and sink the waves of pirate and navy ships that come
for you, for as long as your hull holds. Each wave brings more ships, and bigger ones.

The game's art and sound come from your own copy of the 2008 click-wheel iPod game "Pirates of
the Caribbean" (the .ipg file iTunes kept). They aren't included: you supply the file.

INSTALL
1. Install it from Library -> App Store -> Games on the Q2, or copy the .challengerdeep folder to
   the root of the card. On a Mac, press Cmd-Shift-. in Finder to see dot-folders.
2. Copy your .ipg into .challengerdeep (any file name ending .ipg; it's read, never changed).
3. Eject the card and open Library -> Games -> Challenger Deep.

FROM 1.0
1.0 lived in a .pirates folder. 1.1 still finds the .ipg there, and takes your best score from it
the first time it runs. To tidy up, move the .ipg into .challengerdeep and delete .pirates.

CONTROLS
  Wheel          steer (a full turn of the wheel turns the ship three quarters round)
  Centre         fire both broadsides
  Next / Prev    more sail / less sail (three levels, and none to stop)
  Tap the screen fire the broadside on the side of the ship you tap
  Play/Pause     pause (Return too): Back to sea, or Strike colours to end the game
  Hold Return    leave the game (Q2 Pod's own)

In the menus the wheel moves and Centre picks.

HOW TO PLAY
- Your guns fire sideways, so turn to put an enemy off your side before you fire. The bars at
  the bottom show each side reloading. Enemies do the same.
- Orange arrows at the edge point to ships off screen (red for the big ones).
- Sunk ships leave gold and chests, and sometimes planks (repairs a third of your hull),
  powder kegs (reload twice as fast for 12 s) or gems. Sail over them to pick them up.
- Clearing a wave repairs a quarter of your hull.
- Islands are solid: run aground and you lose speed (and a little hull).

FILES
  q2-challengerdeep    the game
  challengerdeep.sav   your best score and wave, and the ship and sea you last chose
  challengerdeep.log   what the game did, for when something goes wrong
